Install the DiabetesHub display

This page puts the display software onto your M5Stack Core2. You only do this once. It's free, takes about 5 minutes, and you don't need any technical knowledge. Just follow the steps in order.

Not a medical device. The display is a convenience only. Never use it to make treatment decisions. Always check readings on your pump, sensor app or a finger-prick test, and keep your phone app's alarms switched on.
You can't install from this phone, tablet or browser. Please open this page on a laptop or desktop computer, in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge. Tip: send yourself the link so you can open it on your computer. The setup after installing is done on your phone.

Which computer can I use?

Windows laptop or PCin Chrome or Edge
Apple Macin Chrome or Edge, not Safari
Chromebookin Chrome
Phone or iPadcan't install, used for setup later

The steps are the same on every computer. Where Windows and Mac differ, it says so.

You'll need

  • Your M5Stack Core2 display
  • A USB-C cable that carries data. The one that came in the box is best. Some phone charging cables only charge, and won't work.
  • A laptop or desktop computer with Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge

Step by step

1

Open this page in Chrome or Edge, on your computer

If you're reading this in Safari or Firefox, copy the address at the top of the screen and paste it into Chrome or Edge.

Windows Edge is already installed. Mac Download Chrome free from google.com/chrome if you don't have it.

2

Plug the display into the computer

Use the USB-C cable. Plug it straight into the computer, not into a hub or a keyboard. The display may switch on by itself, which is fine.

The display connects to the computer with the USB-C cable

3

Press Connect and install

A small box opens at the top of your screen.

M5Stack Core2

Latest version: checking…

Installing replaces anything already on the display, including any saved Wi-Fi and CareLink sign-in. You'll set those up again in step 7.

4

Choose your display in the list, then press Connect

There's usually only one thing in the list. Its name includes USB, Serial, CH9102 or CP210. Click it, then press Connect.

If the list is empty, see "The list is empty" below.

The name may look a little different on your computer

5

Press Install, tick Erase device, then press Next and Install

  • Press the Install button. It may say Install DiabetesHub display.
  • It asks whether to erase the device. Tick Erase device and press Next.
  • Press Install to confirm.

Erasing clears any old software, so the display starts fresh

6

Wait until it says Installation complete

It takes about 2 minutes. Don't unplug the display or close the page while it's installing. When it says Installation complete, press Next or Close, then unplug the display.

7

Set it up on your phone

Plug the display into a phone charger or leave it on battery. It shows its own Wi-Fi name, starting with M5NS, with a password and a code to scan. Join it from your phone, then add your home Wi-Fi and your CareLink care partner sign-in.

The full setup guide is on diabeteshub.ie, in Devices, Wee Glo.

If something goes wrong

The list is empty, or the display isn't in it

1. Try another cable. This is the most common cause. Many cables only charge. The cable that came with the display works.

2. Plug it straight into the computer, not a hub or keyboard, and try another USB port.

3. Install the USB driver, then restart the computer and try again. A driver is a small free program that lets the computer talk to the display. Download it from M5Stack, the company that makes the display:

  • Windows CH9102 driver for Windows. Open the file and press Install.
  • Mac CH9102 driver for Mac. Open the file and follow the steps. It may show an error at the end, but it still works.
  • Older Core2 displays, bought before 2021, use a different chip. If the driver above doesn't help, get the CP210x driver from M5Stack's download page.

Chromebook No driver is needed. Try another cable or port.

It stopped, or says it failed

Unplug the display, wait 10 seconds, plug it back in, then start again from step 3. Nothing is broken: you can install as many times as you like.

Close any other program that might be using the display, then try again.

The blue button is grey

Your browser can't install. Open this page in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a laptop or desktop computer. Safari, Firefox, phones and iPads can't do this step.

The display screen stays black after installing

Press the small power button on the left side of the display for 2 seconds. If it's still black, charge it for 15 minutes and try again.

Still stuck? Email diabeteshub.ie@gmail.com with a photo of your screen.
